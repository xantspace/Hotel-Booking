## Brand / Voice
- **Tone:** Warm, professional, hospitality-first.
- **Messaging:** Clear, concise copy that sells comfort, value, and convenience.
- **Emphasis:** Serenity (especially regarding the restaurant), accessibility, free inclusions (Wi-Fi, breakfast, parking), and family/business-friendly features.

## Design & UI Guidance
- **Visual Style:** Modern, warm neutrals with one accent color (deep teal or warm orange).
- **Typography:** Clean typography.
- **Hero:** Large hero image.
- **Layout:** Mobile-first, responsive grid, sticky top navigation on scroll.
- **Imagery:** Professional photography showing real rooms and the restaurant. Use a lightbox viewer for the gallery.
- **Trust Badges:** 4.4/101 rating badge, secure booking (HTTPS) indicators, third-party OTA logos in price comparison.
- **Buttons (CTAs):**
  - **Primary:** "Check availability / Book" (Primary color).
  - **Secondary:** "Compare prices / Contact" (Secondary color).
- **Accessibility:** WCAG AA color contrasts, keyboard navigable, alt text for all images.
